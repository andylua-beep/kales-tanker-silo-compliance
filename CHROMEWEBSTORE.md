## Detailed Description
Tanker & Cement Bulker Semi-Trailer Compliance Checker (by KALES) verifies local road weight, axle load, ullage safety, ADR compartment/rollover limits, and tare ROI compliance for fuel tankers, chemical tankers, stainless tankers, and V-type pneumatic dry-bulk cement silos before dispatch or fleet purchase.

Key Features:
1. Geometric Tanker Shell Volume Formulas: Compute exact net tank capacity in liters and cubic meters (m³) for cylindrical (πr²L), elliptical (πabL ≈ 0.785×W×H×L), and square-round (0.82×W×H×L) tanker shells with two standard elliptical dished heads (2 × πWHh/6) and internal baffle/manufacturing loss deductions.
2. Segmented V-Type Bulk Cement Silo Volume & Pneumatic Discharge: Model upper horizontal barrel + N inverted frustum hopper cones + elliptical heads minus fluidization bed/air-chamber allowance (5% default), plus air compressor flow (m³/min), unloading minutes, and 0.2 MPa PED / TSG 21 pressure boundary checks.
3. Multi-Compartment Fuel Tanker, ADR 6.8.2 & UN ECE R111 Stability: Plan 1 to 6 compartments for diesel, gasoline, jet fuel, crude oil, edible oil, bitumen, sulfuric acid, and caustic soda with density-weighted payload, ADR 7,500 L surge-baffle & 20–80% fill checks, ADR 6.8.2.1.28 top rollover coaming envelope, UN ECE R111 23° static rollover stability estimate, and thermal expansion ullage alerts.
4. 28 National & Regional Road Law Compliance Checks: Verify axle, GCW/GVW, length, width, and height compliance for aluminum, carbon steel, and stainless tankers across China (GB 1589-2016), EU (96/53/EC), UK (C&U 44 t), US (23 CFR 658.17), Japan, Australia (NHVR HVNL), Taiwan, Vietnam (Circular 39/2024), Indonesia (PP 55/2012), Philippines (RA 8794), Thailand (DOH 50.5 t), Mexico (NOM-012), Brazil (CONTRAN 882/2021), Colombia (Res. 4100/2004), Peru (D.S. 058-2003-MTC), Chile (D.S. 158 MOP), Tanzania (EAC/TANROADS), East Africa (EAC), Southern Africa (SADC), South Africa (NRTR Reg. 240), Nigeria, Guinea, Ghana, Kazakhstan (EAEU), Russia (PP No. 2200), UAE (138/2023), and Saudi Arabia (RGA), plus ISO 1726 & ECE R58 RUPD design boundaries.
5. Suspension, ETRTO Tyre Ratings & Scaled Side-Elevation Schematic: Verify per-axle, steer, drive, kingpin, and GCW distribution with printable technical spec sheets.
