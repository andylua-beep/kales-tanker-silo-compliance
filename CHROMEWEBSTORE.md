## Detailed Description
Tanker & Cement Bulker Semi-Trailer Compliance Checker (by KALES) verifies local road weight, axle load, ullage safety, and tare ROI compliance for fuel tankers, chemical tankers, stainless tankers, and V-type pneumatic dry-bulk cement silos before dispatch or fleet purchase.

Key Features:
1. Geometric Tanker Shell Volume Formulas: Compute exact net tank capacity in liters and cubic meters (m³) for cylindrical (πr²L), elliptical (πabL ≈ 0.785×W×H×L), and square-round (0.82×W×H×L) tanker shells with two standard elliptical dished heads (2 × πWHh/6) and internal baffle/manufacturing loss deductions.
2. Segmented V-Type Bulk Cement Silo Volume & Pneumatic Discharge: Model upper horizontal barrel + N inverted frustum hopper cones + elliptical heads minus fluidization bed/air-chamber allowance (5% default), plus air compressor flow (m³/min) and unloading minutes for Portland cement and fly ash bulkers.
3. Multi-Compartment Fuel Tanker & Thermal Ullage Safety: Plan 1 to 6 compartments for diesel, gasoline, jet fuel, crude oil, edible oil, bitumen, sulfuric acid, and caustic soda with density-weighted payload, ADR/material compatibility checks, and thermal expansion ullage alerts.
4. 20 National & Regional Road Law Compliance Checks: Verify axle, GCW/GVW, length, width, and height compliance for aluminum, carbon steel, and stainless tankers across China (GB 1589-2016), EU (96/53/EC), US (23 CFR 658.17), Japan (Vehicle Restriction Order), Australia (NHVR HVNL General Mass Limits), Taiwan (Art. 38/79), Vietnam (Circular 39/2024), Indonesia (PP 55/2012 MST 10 t), Peru (D.S. 058-2003-MTC), Chile (D.S. 158 MOP), Tanzania (EAC/TANROADS), East Africa (EAC), Nigeria (ECOWAS), Guinea (ECOWAS), Ghana (L.I. 2180), South Africa (NRTR Reg. 240), Russia (PP No. 2200), UAE (138/2023), and Saudi Arabia (RGA).
5. Suspension, ETRTO Tyre Ratings & Scaled Side-Elevation Schematic: Verify per-axle, steer, drive, kingpin, and GCW distribution with printable technical spec sheets.
